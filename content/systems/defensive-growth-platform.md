## Purpose

The Defensive Growth Platform is the production system required to manage the Defensive Growth strategy after the architecture extended the earlier defensive equity framework to market-level participation.

## System scope

The system supported:

- Live portfolio management for Defensive Growth
- Historical backtests and strategy research
- Portfolio optimizations and rebalance workflows
- Client restriction lists and mandate-specific constraints
- Portfolio monitoring, performance review, and exception handling

## Strategic value

The platform made Defensive Growth operational. It connected the strategy architecture to the production workflows needed for portfolio management, client restrictions, review, and ongoing monitoring.
