## Purpose

The Defensive Equity Platform is the production system required to operate the defensive equity strategies after the portfolio-construction architecture had been designed.

## System scope

The system supported:

- Live portfolio management across defensive equity strategies
- Historical backtests and strategy research
- Portfolio optimizations and production rebalance workflows
- Client restriction lists and mandate-specific implementation constraints
- Portfolio monitoring, review, and exception handling

## Strategic value

The platform turned the defensive equity architecture into an operating capability. It connected research, portfolio construction, implementation, and monitoring so the strategies could be managed live rather than remaining only a research framework.
