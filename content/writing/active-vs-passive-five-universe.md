## Core claim

The active-versus-passive question is not one question. It is five, depending on universe, implementation style, and the integrity of the benchmark itself.

## Scope

eVestment data across five equity universes for an official-sector institution. Examined when and where active management has paid, including China benchmark-integrity considerations and a quant-versus-fundamental split.

## Process

Taken through compliance to a distribution-ready package, then re-tailored for a second institutional client. Demonstrates the translation layer: one research core, multiple client-ready artifacts.

## Implication

Generic active-versus-passive debate is the wrong frame. The usable answer is universe-specific and constraint-specific.

## Intellectual honesty

Includes sections on where robustness is weaker than the headline and where claims should be hedged. That discipline is deliberate, not accidental.
