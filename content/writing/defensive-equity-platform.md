## Scope

Architected the Defensive Equity strategy framework spanning U.S., International, Small Cap, and Emerging Markets strategies. The long-only, low-volatility framework was designed to deliver competitive downside protection across market cycles.

## Architecture

Risk-allocation based construction with multidimensional risk balancing. Structural objective of low correlation to traditional long-only portfolios while preserving capacity and tradability.

## Production infrastructure

Built the infrastructure required to manage the strategies in production: backtests, optimizations, client restriction lists, portfolio monitoring, and repeatable review workflows.

## Commercialization

Strategies were commercialized within the multi-asset platform and integrated into broader product positioning.
