## Scope

Architected Defensive Equity platform spanning U.S., International, Small Cap, and Emerging Markets strategies. Long-only, low-volatility framework designed to deliver competitive downside protection across market cycles.

## Architecture

Risk-allocation based construction with multidimensional risk balancing. Structural objective of low correlation to traditional long-only portfolios while preserving capacity and tradability.
